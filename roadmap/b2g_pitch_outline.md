# B2G Pitch: TNAAI Solutions x StartupTN

## Problem Statement
- StartupTN receives thousands of TANSEED applications.
- Many are ineligible or poor quality, leading to high human review overhead.
- Founders struggle with the complexity of government guidelines.

## Our Solution
- **Agentic Pre-screening**: Instantly verify entity, sector, and financial eligibility based on TN Deep Tech Policy.
- **RAG-Powered Drafting**: Generate high-quality application drafts grounded in the official TANSEED RFP.

## Value Proposition for Government
1. **Efficiency**: Reduce manual screening time by 70%.
2. **Quality**: Higher quality submissions lead to faster disbursement.
3. **Accessibility**: Language-agnostic (English/Tamil) support for founders across the state.

## Pilot Metrics (Planned)
- Time saved per application.
- Accuracy of automated vs. human screening.
