# B2G Pitch: TNAAI Solutions x StartupTN (Version 1)

## 1. Executive Summary
Empowering Tamil Nadu's startup ecosystem through Agentic AI. We reduce the administrative burden of grant processing by 70% while improving the quality of submissions.

## 2. The Bottleneck: Grant Processing at Scale
- **Volume**: 2000+ applications per TANSEED cohort.
- **Friction**: 40% of applications are rejected due to basic eligibility mismatches.
- **Cost**: Thousands of man-hours spent on manual pre-screening.

## 3. The Solution: TNAA GovTech Concierge
- **Agentic Pre-screening**: Direct integration with TN Deep Tech Policy rules.
- **Intelligent Drafting**: RAG-powered assistant that aligns founder vision with government requirements.
- **Multi-lingual Support**: English and Tamil support to ensure inclusivity across all districts.

## 4. Operational Excellence
- **Security**: Data residency in India, compliant with state IT policies.
- **Scalability**: Capable of handling 10,000+ simultaneous screening requests.
- **Transparency**: Clear audit trail for every eligibility decision.

## 5. The Pilot Roadmap
- **Month 1**: Closed Beta with 5 Startups (Current Phase).
- **Month 2**: Integration testing with StartupTN sandbox (Proposed).
- **Month 3**: State-wide roll-out for next TANSEED cohort.

## 6. Vision for the State
Building the "AI Concierge for TN" - a single window where every founder's query is answered instantly by an agent trained on the state's entire policy framework.
