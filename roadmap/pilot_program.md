# TNAA Solutions Pilot Program

## Objective
Validate the RAG pipeline's accuracy and the overall user experience of the TANSEED Eligibility Checker with 5 early-stage startups in Tamil Nadu.

## Target Startups
- Sector: AgriTech, DeepTech, Sustainability, SaaS.
- Stage: Idea to MVP.
- Locations: Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy, Tirunelveli.

## Outreach Strategy
- Direct LinkedIn outreach to founders.
- Partnership with college incubation cells (e.g., IITM IC, Anna University CED).

## Pilot Deliverables
1. **User Feedback**: Qualitative interview after using the tool.
2. **Accuracy Matrix**: RAG-generated eligibility results vs. founder's manual assessment.
3. **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of pilot users who would pay for the draft assistance.
