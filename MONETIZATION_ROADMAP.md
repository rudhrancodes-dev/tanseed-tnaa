# TNAA Monetization & Market Validation Roadmap

**Status:** Prototype Complete
**Phase:** Market Validation & Revenue Generation

## 1. Monetization Strategy (Tamil Nadu focus)

### Tier 1: B2C Preparation Service (Quick Cash)
- **Target:** Startups applying for TANSEED (thousands per cycle).
- **Product:** "AI-Ready" application package.
- **Price:** ₹1,999 per application.
- **Value:** RAG engine ensures compliance with 100% of official guidelines, drastically reducing rejection rates.

### Tier 2: B2B for Incubators (Recurring Revenue)
- **Target:** Local incubators (Madurai, Coimbatore, Trichy).
- **Product:** Licensing the platform for internal grant management.
- **Price:** ₹10,000/month.
- **Value:** Automates the "first-pass" eligibility filter for cohort applications.

### Tier 3: B2G White-Label (High Value)
- **Target:** StartupTN (TANSIM).
- **Product:** Official applicant pre-screening portal.
- **Model:** Licensing or service contract.
- **Value:** 70% reduction in manual administrative review time.
